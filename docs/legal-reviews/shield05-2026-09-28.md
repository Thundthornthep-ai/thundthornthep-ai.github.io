## Substantive corrections

- Separate leave entitlement, employer wage payment and social-security benefits. Remove unsupported social-security ceiling/45-day arithmetic and combined-benefit total. Include conditional additional child-care leave and 50% wage payment.
- Separate section 17 notice from 17/1 payment and section 118 severance. Worked notice example with payday on the first: notice on 15 June reaches the 1 July pay date and takes effect on 1 August, not 1 July. No unsupported final notice-payment total.
- Preserve six severance brackets and fixed-term exceptions. Commission treatment depends on actual wage characteristics. Do not apply a universal 90-day average.
- Correct working-day leave units and paid-leave limits. Preserve working-time, consent and overtime eligibility exceptions. Do not equate all military service with statutory mobilisation leave.
- Remove mandatory three-warning and employee-signature claims. State section 119 notice-of-grounds and one-year warning validity accurately. Section 146 is a regulatory fine of up to 20,000 baht, not an automatic fixed criminal fine.
- Distinguish unfair dismissal from severance eligibility. Remove unsupported case predictions, probation-as-avoidance advice, automatic employer ownership of all IP, and blanket foreign-worker visa/ratio/permit-termination rules.
- FAQ reorganisation answer confines sections 121/122 to their statutory conditions. Resignation and transitional leave questions require case facts, not categorical imported doctrines.
